ABOUT THE ARTIST

I don't just paint
what I see.

My work explores emotions, everyday moments and the beauty of imperfect things. Through painting and illustration, I try to transform simple ideas into visual stories.

From an idea
to a piece of art.

Every artwork begins with a simple idea and slowly takes shape through observation, experimentation and color.

02

I explore shapes, composition and movement before creating the final piece.

03

COLOR

Colors and textures create the mood and give the artwork its personality.

04

The idea becomes a finished artwork, ready to tell its own story.
